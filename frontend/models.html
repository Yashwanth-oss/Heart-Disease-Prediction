Data Integrity & De-Duplication Protocol

Standard public datasets for heart disease often replicate the 303 original Cleveland patients across 1,025 rows (70.5% duplicate records). Without deduplication, standard train-test splitting leaks identical patient profiles into validation folds, producing artificially inflated (~98-100%) test scores that fail in real clinical settings. CardioAI enforces strict patient deduplication and 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation to guarantee authentic real-world clinical generalization.
